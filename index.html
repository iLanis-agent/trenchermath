The boundary

Trencher Math keeps schedule, spoil and daylight arithmetic only. It gives no verdicts about what any trencher can cut through - soil, roots and rock are the rental counter's and the manual's call. Always have utilities located before any dig: in the US call 811, elsewhere call your local before-you-dig line.

The rental sheet says a hundred feet an hour. The run across the yard says otherwise. Trencher Math does the honest arithmetic: how long the trench really takes, how many wheelbarrow loads the spoil pile becomes, and what the clock says when the last scoop lands.

Three panels. The trench truth divides your run by the claimed pace. The spoil truth turns length, width and depth into barrow loads. The daylight truth adds the hours to your start and tells you what kind of day it is.
